Privacy Policy

Home Dashboard is a personal project built and used by a single individual for private household use. It is not distributed, sold, or made available to other users.

The application requests read-only access to a single Google Calendar (scope: calendar.readonly) solely to display upcoming events on a private, local-network dashboard. No calendar data, or any other personal data, is shared, sold, transmitted to third parties, or used for any purpose other than this display.

Access credentials are stored only on the operator's own private server and are never transmitted elsewhere.
